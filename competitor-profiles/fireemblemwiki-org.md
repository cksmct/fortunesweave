## Why this profile exists

This site overturned a conclusion made earlier in the project: the claim that no consolidated weapon list exists anywhere. Fandom has no such page (the parse request returns missingtitle), but fireemblemwiki.org does, so the correct statement is that one wiki has it and another does not.

## Confirmed coverage (2026-09-28)

- List of weapons in Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave: 101 weapon and spell entries with type, level, might, weight, hit, critical, curse, range, uses, worth and effect text.
- List of characters in Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave, and a main game page describing the story as two parts split by a timeskip.
- List of classes in Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave (carried by Fandom under the same name; both wikis are useful for cross-checking).

## Strengths

- Authoritative series-wide coverage and stable permalinks.
- Structured tables that survive parsing, which is why our weapon table exists at all.

## Weaknesses

- No dated rows, no tools, no guide-shaped answers, and a wikitext interface that reads as a reference rather than a companion.
- Coverage is uneven by page: some lists are complete, others sit as stubs.

## Implications for fortunesweave.online

- Cite them, do not copy them: our weapon rows carry a source label and a check date, which their table does not.
- Any claim that we are the only structured database is now false, so the honest positioning is sourced and dated rows plus tools, not exclusivity.
