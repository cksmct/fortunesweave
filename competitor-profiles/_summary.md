## Landscape overview

Three layers. A commercial guides wiki (Game8) and a media wiki (IGN) own the head terms with hourly updates. A long-running community wiki (fireemblemwiki.org) owns structured series data and is the only place with a complete weapon and spell table. Behind them sits a layer of independent sites that appeared around launch week, two of which describe themselves as source-grounded while citing no sources at all.

## Key takeaways

1. Volume alone does not win: two competitors publish dozens of pages that refuse to publish a single number, and one says so explicitly.
2. Per-row sourcing is still unclaimed. Two sites call themselves source-grounded and neither links a source, so our row-level label plus date remains the clearest differentiator.
3. The weapon gap is closed, and it was closed by correcting a misjudgement: Fandom has no weapon list, fireemblemwiki.org does.
4. No competitor in this set ships interactive tools, so the seven tools and the schematic map stay uncontested.
5. The remaining gap is systems coverage, not data coverage: crop farming, animal mounts, gambits and battalions, meals, Crests and abilities, factions, deities and post-game content are documented elsewhere and absent here.

## Opportunities, ranked by value per unit of sourcing effort

1. Crop farming, animal mounts and the stable: one source publishes thresholds we can cross-check.
2. Gambits and battalions plus hero talents: absent from every guide checked in depth, central to battle outcomes.
3. Meals: Fextralife keeps a Meals category and we only cover drink recipes.
4. Factions overview: we hold 111 named faction members with no page that groups them, while a competitor lists 21 factions one line each.
5. Post-game and Merge Causality: thin everywhere, so a sourced version would be first.
